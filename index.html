Important safety note

Temperature and humidity are environmental indicators, not a food-safety certificate. Actual drying time depends on fruit type, slice thickness, dryer design, airflow and weather. Use a validated food-preservation procedure, hygienic handling and a suitable moisture or water-activity check. A trained facilitator must approve every batch before packaging.

COMMUNITY KNOWLEDGE
🟠

Marula

Communities have long used marula fruit in foods and beverages. Discuss local selection, seasonal availability and traditional uses with knowledgeable community members.

Preservation tip: sort carefully, wash with potable water and use a tested drying or processing method appropriate for the fruit.
🥭

Mango

Mango can be sliced and dried to create a shelf-stable snack when the process is controlled and the product is adequately dried.

Preservation tip: use even slice thickness and avoid overcrowding trays to support consistent airflow.
🍌

Banana

Ripe but sound bananas can be prepared as dried slices. Community preferences can guide ripeness, flavour and product presentation.

Preservation tip: remove damaged fruit and keep preparation surfaces and utensils clean.

Why value addition matters

Preserving surplus fruit can help farmers sell beyond the fresh-harvest window, reduce spoilage and create products for local markets. Actual income depends on quality, demand, packaging, transport and operating costs.

This prototype does not process payments, make sales or verify food compliance. Price fields are planning records only.
